For my data visualisation project, I am collecting my daily Spotify streaming statistics and translating them onto individual vinyl designs.

I have collected 5 stats daily: the number of minutes I listened to music, the number of tracks streamed, the number of different artists I listened to,
the number of different albums I listened to, and the top genre for that day. These 5 categories influence different parts of the vinyl record.

Number of minutes = how fast the record is spinning (its velocity).
Number of different albums = the length of the first groove in the vinyl.
Number of different artists = the length of the second groove in the vinyl.
Number of tracks streamed = the length of the third groove in the vinyl.
Top genre = the colour of the inner ring of the vinyl.

Observations from recording my own data:
1) I don't listen to music without having earphones / headphones as days when I had forgotton them during my commute or hadn't charged them, I didn't listen to anything.
2) My genres really fluctuate depending on new albums. Usually, hyperpop isn't close to my top genres; however an artist I used to listen to released a new album after a 4 year break a week into this project, and so this has been a frequent genre in the last month of collecting my data.
